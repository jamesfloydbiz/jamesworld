The realization that a random passerby is living

a life as vivid & complex as your own.

A feeling that happens only when you put down your phone,

when you loan your attention to the present

and awaken from the slumber of distraction.

When you let the light of the world touch you without refraction,

in that way each of us has had an interaction

a moment without fight where our stories swirled.

Such is the way of serendipity and Sonder.

So as I ponder the time we spent and queries of life,

I am grateful to you for being part of the series.
