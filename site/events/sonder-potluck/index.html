The Sonder Series Potluck

When Saturday, June 13 · 5:30–7:30 PM
Where A park near you (NYC)
Who All 100 Sonder Series interviewees + friends

A gathering with invites to all 100 interviews from the Sonder Series.

One hundred interviews. Five boroughs. One potluck. After thirty-two days walking 180 miles across the city and asking strangers three questions, the idea was simple: bring everyone into the same field at golden hour and see who shows up.
