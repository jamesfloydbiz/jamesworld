Job Demand — what Alpha is hiring for most

Pulled live from the Crossover board (June 2026). 67 roles total across all their entities; 38 are Alpha.

The one-line read: by raw headcount, demand is overwhelmingly Guides (classroom coaching) across ~20 cities — they're staffing a multi-city land grab. By seniority & pay, the hiring clusters in admissions/enrollment, Deans of Parents (retention), and campus operations — the launch machine. There is no “Chief of Staff” or “Head of Special Projects” by title; the closest senior generalist roles are VP / Regional VP of Campus Operations, Head of Marketing, and Director of Academics.

Where the demand concentrates

  • Teaching / coaching (biggest volume): Guide, Guide K-3, Guide II, Lead Guide, Lead Guide II, Elite Guide, PreK, High School — dozens of postings across ~20 cities. This is the #1 demand.
  • Enrollment / launch: Directors of Admissions (SF, Eastern, Central, Seattle/Park City), Admissions Associates (NYC, SF), Senior Admissions Specialists (remote). Standing up new cities.
  • Parent experience / retention: Dean of Parents (Miami, $400K), Associate Dean of Parents (~18 cities), Associate Dean of Parents – CA.
  • Campus operations leadership: VP of Campus Operations, Regional VP of Campus Operations (Central TX, NW), Campus Operations Specialist, Campus Logistics Coordinator, Campus Coordinator.
  • Marketing / narrative: Head of Media ($400K), Head of Marketing ($200K).
  • Academics / specialists: Director of Academics ($400K — 2HL's “academic north star”), Reading Specialists (in-person, many cities).
  • Tech: Principal AI Engineer, Technical Team Lead, L2 Customer Support Engineer (remote).

Parents' Q&A — from the live info session

Open “Alpha School questions from parents” (Drive) ↗

Working copy of your doc. Original “Alpha.School” left untouched. Highlights below.

Numbers & facts they gave

  • 97% reenrollment rate.
  • 80,000 applicants for guide roles last year; guides hold a bachelor's, not required to be credentialed (hired for coaching).
  • Guide-to-student ratios: ~1:5 elementary, ~1:10 middle, ~1:15 high school.
  • New campuses usually open with 40–50 students (SF ~60). They won't open a campus until 25 students are enrolled.
  • $100 application fee; rolling admissions; a shadow day is required to apply.
  • Mastery-based (90%+ to advance), no letter grades. ~50 total graduates — “all became entrepreneurs or went to their top-choice university.”
  • Subject/learning specialists = a national remote team; reading specialists are on-campus.
  • Alpha Anywhere (homeschool) = grades 3–8. They license the engine to private schools (don't franchise; not taking new licensees right now).
  • Financial aid offered at larger campuses. Average ~99th percentile on MAP (their claim).

Expansion notes dropped in the chat

  • The Woodlands = only Houston campus this fall (under renovation). Chicago renovating, opens PK-8. OC expanding into Irvine/Newport. Greenwich CT campus for the fall. Marin/Sausalito still hunting real estate.
  • K-12 in Austin; high school expanding to SF, Santa Barbara, NYC, Miami, OKC. New campuses usually open K-8 then grow to K-12.
  • Toronto admissions contact given: Kevin.ly@alpha.school.
