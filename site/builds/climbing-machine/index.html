I'm a Climbing Machine

by James Floyd

“I want to climb to the top of the mountain today,” said James.

“All the way?” he heard his teacher Floyd say.

2

It was a big mountain, but James was trying to be brave.

From the effort he gave his 2 hour learning apps he had found that he could do hard things.

Besides, this was an Alpha School life skills workshop and he knew the challenge each one brings.

4

James responded, “I will be a climbing machine.”

His little legs were bouncing nervously in his blue jeans.

6

James watched other hikers as they went up and up and up the trail until they looked bite size.

8

Was he even the right size to do this?

Would he have the might to rise to this?

10

Floyd reminded everyone “Remember, at Alpha we are Limitless!”

“I really want to finish this,” thought James, his heart beating fast.

12

As the class stood at the start of the path, James looked up to the top.

He told his classmates “I’ll go in the back. Let’s swap spots.”

14

They started to climb. Up and up. He didn’t want to whine so he stopped to tie his shoe.

Then he faked a cough. “Maybe I can pretend I have the flu,” he thought.

16

Floyd knew what James was up to. “How you doing, buddy? You’re looking a little pale.”

Floyd pulled James to the side of the trail, where they flopped onto a patch of soft grass.

With a huge breath James let his feelings go.

“My legs ache and I don’t want to fail. I want to keep up with the class!”

18

James felt like he was in a funk.

“You’ve won already! We’re done with a little chunk!” Floyd encouraged.

20

But as they sat looking up to the top, James said “Maybe I can try again another day.”

Floyd looked at James and said, “we make big goals at Alpha School, and I think you can reach them. I’m right here all the way too.”

22

Floyd knew James loved games.

So he pointed to a big rock a little bit up the trail.

“Sometimes, I like to make hard things into games so they’re fun. I bet you it’s one-hundred steps to that rock.”

This time James didn’t balk, he got up and started walking, counting each step.

24

When they were done, Floyd pointed at a big tree up the trail and asked “How many steps if you run there?”

James took a deep breath of air, guessed, and went off. Up and up and up.

26

When they reached the tree James looked at Floyd and said “I’m a climbing machine.”

He was keen to keep going.

28

Chunk by chunk, James’ funk subsided.

His legs ached more, but he felt lighter. They went up and up and up. Higher and higher.

30

James finally pointed at the top and said “Bet you can’t stop me! I’m a climbing machine!!”

The sun gleamed, and James beamed.

On the summit James looked out as far as he could see.

Past the big tree, the big rock, past the spot he worried he’d fail, past the start of the trail, and out to another mountaintop. It all looked itty bitty.

He pointed and told Floyd, “I am going to the top there.”

Floyd asked, “All the way?”

James looked him in the eye and said, “I’m a climbing machine.”

The End

Written for the kids (and the grown-ups)
still learning that hard things
come one chunk at a time.
