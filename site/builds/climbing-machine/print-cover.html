Alpha School is a new kind of school that teaches all of the academics in 2 hours in the morning, then in the afternoons the kids learn different life skills to prepare them for their futures.

This story follows James, as he takes on the challenge of climbing a mountain in one of those life skills workshops.

It is a story about hard things, and how a guide who believes in you can help you believe anything is possible.

The three commitments of Alpha School

Love school “Alpha students love school—it’s engaging, inspiring, and built for them.”
Learn 2x in 2hrs “Alpha classes learn twice as fast as their peers and rank in the top 1% nationwide.”
Learn life skills “Alpha students spend afternoons developing life skills and exploring their passions.”
