36 Questions

The questions that build closeness — done right.

Two people, taking turns, through three sets that go deeper as you climb.

I was enjoying these questions with my person, but not the 36questions site — so I made a better one.

Credit for most of the content goes to Dr. Arthur Aron and his research team, who created these questions in a 1997 study — and to Mandy Len Catron, whose New York Times “Modern Love” essay sent them around the world.

— James

Questions from Aron et al. (1997), “The Experimental Generation of Interpersonal Closeness.” Popularized by Mandy Len Catron, The New York Times, 2015.
