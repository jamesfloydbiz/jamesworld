Questions owners ask

Before you sign up.

What does it cost me?
No sign-up fee and no software. Families pay with a Visa card, so you pay your usual card processing fee on the sale, the same as any other card.
Which of my classes can families pay for with ESA?
Arizona's ESA handbook allows things like sports leagues, sports camps and a gym membership for the student. Structured instruction, such as a weekly homeschool PE class with a named coach, is the safest fit. Open gym and drop-in play are more often denied as recreation. Families can check any class in the Pathfinder app before they buy.
We already take ClassWallet. Do we have to stop?
No. Keep it. Pathfinder is one more way for families to pay you, and it's the fastest one for you.
What if the state rejects a purchase later?
That's between the family and Pathfinder, not you. If the app pre-approved the purchase, Pathfinder covers it up to its guarantee limits. You keep the sale either way.
We're a franchise. Can we still join?
Yes. Nothing changes in your point of sale. If your franchisor reviews local marketing, we'll send the kit for approval before anything goes up.
How many ESA families are there?
More than 100,000 Arizona students have an ESA this school year, and thousands of families already use the Pathfinder app.
