27 pieces

Poems written between the middle of 2025 and 2026, handwritten on whatever was nearby — torn notebook pages, the back of a receipt, a stranger's napkin. The handwriting is part of the piece, so each one is photographed as-is rather than retyped. Themes run across wisdom, struggle, love, manhood, fight, peace, energy, sleep, contradictions, possibility, eyes, home, abundance, death, and the small honest observations in between. One older piece, I Come From — written in high school — is set in type instead. Click any thumbnail to read.
