How to read it

Each band is money moving in one year, and its width is the dollar amount. With Who paid, a band takes the color of whoever first paid it: blue federal, orange state, green local, gold families, pink donors. With Who decides, it takes the color of whoever controls how much moves along it.

Each dollar is drawn once, at the point it leaves the person or government that pays it. School choice money is shown as state money, because states pay for it, even when it ends up as private school tuition. Gifts to scholarship groups that are repaid as state tax credits are left out of donations for the same reason.

Years do not all match. Public school money is FY2024, the latest full Census count. Choice programs and 529s are 2025-26. Some donation figures rest on older studies. Every band says its year.

Sources

  • Public school revenue and spending, national, by state and by district: U.S. Census Bureau, 2024 Annual Survey of School System Finances.
  • School choice programs: EdChoice spending share rankings and The ABCs of School Choice 2026.
  • Federal tax-credit opt-in status: EdChoice's state map (November 2026), with New York and New Mexico added from news reports. Tracked also by Ballotpedia.
  • Private school, preschool and day care spending: Bureau of Economic Analysis personal consumption data, 2025.
  • Pre-K: NIEER State of Preschool 2025; Head Start appropriations as reported by First Five Years Fund.
  • 529 accounts: ISS Market Intelligence, as reported by PLANADVISER. Yearly K-12 withdrawals are not published anywhere and are estimated.
  • Donations: Giving USA, Candid foundation data, NAIS, and studies of PTA and school foundation revenue.
