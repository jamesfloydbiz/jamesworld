One dashboard for the house

Where every student is, where they are going, and when they are due back. Built for the Head of Student Life role at Founders School.

  • Headcount first. Five numbers that add up to the roster.
  • Nobody goes alone. A sign-out is a pair, and the buddy has to accept.
  • Everyone has a calendar, so everyone has a position. School, workouts, practices, the Saturday outing. The board flags anyone who is off it.
  • Families see their own child. Their week, the Sunday call, the note after Town Hall.
