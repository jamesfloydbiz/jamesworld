A sign-out that takes ten seconds

Scan the QR, tap who you are, tap your buddy, say where and why. The buddy accepts, and the RA board shows both of them on a map with a time they are due back. Built for the Head of Student Life role at Founders School.

  • Nobody goes alone. A sign-out is a pair, and the buddy has to accept before it starts.
  • Headcount first. The board opens on how many are in the house, not on a list of trips.
  • Due back, not just gone. Travel each way plus time there gives a time, and the board flags due-soon and overdue against it.
  • Students can write in. Requests and feedback go straight to the RA between Town Halls.
