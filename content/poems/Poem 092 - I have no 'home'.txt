         I   have no 'home'

   I travel where I wish to conquer
   Light as the wind, but punishing as lightning
   Ferocity bubbles in my soul because where I stand
   how I stand, what I stand for is all that is mine
   I do not settle for fine

   My voice echoes now towards the mountain tops
like thunderclaps. The mountaineers of our
time hear my voice as though one of
their own breaks through the storm. They remember
perhaps they are ahead, but that I work like
I have no bed. They know the feeling
and worry perhaps I'll leave them one day reeling
