Fuel

        It is you against you
      but sometimes others intervene
      Maybe you gave them an opening, maybe you did not
      regardless that is  Fuel

  That errant look or  the deep pain
  Not bottled up, but captured inside you
  You'll reflect on it, learn  from it
   and take the energy as Fuel

  Like a mule that crosses mountains and many miles
  you'll continue on the journey unbroken
  Tough, diligent and strong, plodding along
  For a mule, setback is just Fuel

  Take MJ, or  Nadal absolute jewels
  For them, as you see in their eyes
  Their achievements are inevitable, because the goal is the work
  anything in their way is just Fuel

  For the fire inside you
  a consuming ever growing hunger
  although it will sometimes cool
  everything that comes is just Fuel.

  So know this pain is purposeful with reflection
  Learn from the pain, go inwards towards it
  For the light teaches you less than the darkness
  Change the Fuel to energy and in the end
        all will be seen.
