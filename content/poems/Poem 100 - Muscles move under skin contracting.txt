Muscles move under skin contracting,
pulling as they will their weight to move,
Blood pumps its power to the beat of
the heart. Electricity races its blazing speed
to its destination and cells live and die endlessly.

In unison the bodies systems run, the bacteria of the
gut breaking down the fuel in the stomach, Breath
wheeshes into the lungs as they stretch in expansion
the wind of exhalation whistling as what's needed is
filtered out.

The skin soaks in the sun and the eyes
take colors, movement, and crazy details into the brain to
be analyzed, ears hear waves echoes and balance
the body, fine tuned to the miniscule rhythyms of life
