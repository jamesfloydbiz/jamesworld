   Horns honk, tires screech, music plays, helicopters hum

Steel and Stone structures with fluorescent so bright

A world that feels opposite. Nature feels out of place
   comfort covering us like a weighted blanket yet
we keep creating these boxes of bricks.

   We live in a world of solutions stacked,
      solving side effects some other 'fix' made

   we dare design with our kid kids in mind.
