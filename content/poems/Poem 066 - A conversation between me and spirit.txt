   A conversation between me and spirit

      Inwards I go towards the spirit that knows
      on this journey I bring a disconnection that stings
      an antsyness I feel, so here I kneel
         dear spirit show me, it is you who knows me

      Inwards I go  towards the spirit that knows
      I offer this hunger and examine it with you
      this impatience I feel is a feeling untrue
      You, the spirit, will take me as you do

      So Inwards I go towards the spirit that knows
      I am here and I am open to the greatness that is near
      I will let you, Spirit, lead with no fear
         This is no time to listen with ear, but rather with heart

         So Inwards I go towards the spirit I am
      Let loose the dam of the mind, release the water of peace
      Water me as though I am parched of you
      My hands, my body, my mind, my thoughts yours

      The spirit is me and I am it
      I kneel on the steps to the halls of history
      I prepare to carve myself out of a quarry
      For the spirit may lead me on that dual path it takes
      Down into the pits of darkness with a hammer and chisel
      But I know, I trust that I am a painting on Spirits easel
         I must be patient of mind, so the spirit may show me
               The way aligned
