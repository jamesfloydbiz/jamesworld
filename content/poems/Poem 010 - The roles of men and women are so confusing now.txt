                                              Dec 21, 25
      The roles of men and women are so confusing now
   Women make more than men, more schooled too
On top of that to  provide a house, cars, water, chow
Takes money more than most have  even if they pooled to
Moreover men don't often talk, no rites of passage
or  coming of age. Many judged for having rage.
Biological  aggression, wants and needs passed down
So many men just trying not to drown, trapped by frowns
A search for meaning and as a sage said many distract
themselves with pleasure, when in the past we hunted
and searched for treasure, even that now is sport only.
So what makes a man now? How can we find meaning?
I find some in aggressive pursuits, and being a gentleman, I
work to be aware, and care for those who don't fare well.
I dare dwell on this because meaning gives  life and
makes strife doable. It is a cleaning of mind with filter
of a soul's work, No tolls lurk when chasing that.
So here I am sat, on my mat  dreaming of a day
      where I have captured the art of meaning
