I want you to imagine a late night
sitting by a  lake or ocean, a quiet calm upon you
      Deep  Breath, lights off
see the moons path across the water
light reflecting, rippling all the way to the horizon

It is the dancing of flames, the way this twist and jump
How they curve around logs, and warm you up.  Fire  is
only built with the energy of lightning, the oxygen in our air
and fuel like  dry wood. But man when it catches, what a pyre
               Beautiful

I think of mother  monkeys and how their babies cling on
little hands, small mouths, but a  little soul alight

A defiant elegance of love made strong
