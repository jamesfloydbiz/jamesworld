  Sitting in the shade as the birds chirp
  on a log, the feeling of greatness in the air
  there is power here, spirits call me
  Under my feet is a solid ground
   I remember the men who have walked here
  Rare, today I am he, I am found, I am mere

  There is a buzzing of bees and wind in the trees
  The clouds move by and ruins abide
        Stone after stone artfully placed
  decorated with  love and concentrating power
        this is the center of the universe
        The wounds to it I nurse

  So I sit by a cenote, a spiritual setting
  Significant enough they offered themselves in sacrifice
  I myself am seeing the power of the place, fitting
  soft rustles in the bushes, shade, and sounds of the people
        I sigh as I take it all in

  I feel the power seep into me as I reflect
  for after all our reality is chosen by us alone
  The nature, the kindness it refills and refreshes
  Music plays, the sun rays dance through the leaves
        yet again I breathe

  As I journey through history I think about
  how those who give this more heed come away different
  Tourists so lost in games of no consequence. As they
  walk the soil that is real wrought with blood and toil
  of those who lived back then.

        Eventually I  sit beneath a world wonder
  and ponder power, pleasure, purpose before I
  venture back to the cities of glass and metal.
  This has the purity of a flower petal and the
   strength of the earth.
        I am yet again grateful for this adventure since birth
