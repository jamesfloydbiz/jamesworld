Blank Page, countless thoughts
Refrigerator hums, leaves scatter
Body creaks, eyes water
Sitting alone, in my "home"

Wrinkled clothes, messy hair
Mind awake, trying to care
Let the pen flow, let it all go

Breathe deep, as relaxation seeps
farther and farther from the noise
it is never quiet, hard to have poise
Airplanes, ear rings, neighbor walks, I balk

   In a world so loud, it is hard to be proud
   of anything but peace
   I want to change it all, but it feels like
      if that, then I must  fall
   from grace, in a race to the top

Drop my values, ignore evil
Why oh why, is it always steel on steel
a war for goodness fought alone
by the monsters that have chosen earth to be home

Too many times is there kids that are shot
Biomes that are rocked, status or money got
instead of what really matters.
         After all, Thats why we're here
I cannot talk about me without knowing my problems are mere
drops in a great ocean full of tears
It seems that on and on we will go, a cycle I fear
Pain begets pain as much as I strain
against this great stain on the beauty of us.
This worry is not new, but nor is it old a unique one
         to those who actually care

   So answer me this my dear world...
Because try as I might sometimes I don't see the light
         Answer  me this
Is it worth the fight?
