In an empty room I sit.
reflecting on the work.
Sometimes its like a tomb
but one day it will be a womb
For now I rhyme. and toil
and ever push along.

I think about my mom
and her fight  to belong
in this  world she tried
but eventually she died
you would think I cried
instead she said "go on"

I move forward like most
fighting the ghosts.
I lead with love
Give with all my heart
or at least I try
sometimes I worry I am a beast
a lion that roars, and also scares.
  All I pray is that they know I care
Perhaps I am like a storm
  one  that ebbs and flows
or that tide that comes & goes
Maybe even I am like  many things
     a Frankenstein  by design
Parts from the  best
Stitched together  and dressed
but with the best comes
the worst, a darkness I despise.
It spies and plots
rotting my mind.
In these empty rooms I wait
for my time to come.
