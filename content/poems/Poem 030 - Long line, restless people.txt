   Long line, restless people
   wasted hours, pained  smiles
   crossed arms, shifting feet
    as we all hurry up  and wait

Appointments late, crying babies
      Few  staff, many clients
      Loud AC, Ticking clock
Finite lives and pressing timelines make some balk

Outdated Government, issuing permits
irresponsible development, necessitates cars
Lives  taken         , communities broken
Because the money has many people roped in

Inflation growing , expenses up
   media  brewed, culture stewed
   desperation flowing, rinse  repeat
So in that way goes the tide, despite many who've tried
