Music drifting into the breeze
Sun shining down out of a big blue sky
People walk by their words floating as they will happily
Hard to be unhappy as the world fills you with wonder

Pickleballs pop as shoes & scrape the ground lightly
birds chirp and play and green open grass breathes
Bikes gears click as riders pause to see the view

I sit on a green painted picnic table and
pen down my observations. Patiently waiting for
peering onlookers. Curious ones whos story I can share.
Rare as it may be, I'd rather Let others choose

In the beginning as always the preservation insticts
So many eyes on me what will 'the tribe' think
I wonder though why those feelings come calling
A full fight or flight from fear and yet keep calling forward

Action over anything. Energy put out is energy
transformed. Perhaps

You see failure is effort forwards and better than
fantasizing over outcomes you haven't done the work for.
As I feel the sun on my face I think about how
the two types of successful people are missionaries or craftsmen.

Some laugh now, but when visions are realized we doers
get our laughs then. That's winning
