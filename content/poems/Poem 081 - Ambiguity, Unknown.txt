         Ambiguity, Unknown     Nov 28, 29       2025
   Like the pathway of what's next, unshown

Feelings and thoughts swirl in the cauldron of my stomach
My psyche wanders through the labyrinth of my mind
This grind I'm on directionless if there is no purpose.
I ponder this 'meaning' others have, so sure of serenity
as they plod down the path in the direction of their decision
I am doing _____ for _____. So then what am I
doing? These white walls of this beach house teach
how focus on one thing, the discipline, a life's work gets
the results of compounding. So then how do I decide?
What will be my life's work? For if I want to change
something big it must be strived for. Leaves rustle
Tree branches blow, birds glide in the everchanging winds
Thoughts cycle through, problems meaningful to me, ways of the greats
have gotten off the streets. I feel lost as if I am tangled
in sheets.

All I want is to know          that what I'm doing
matters. Doesn't even really matter if it flatters.
But that it improves the world, and will be enough for my life.
oh what strife it is to be surrounded by those who fly
while I look over yonder towards when it'll die.
wondering if by then I'll have found the yin to this yang.
The pangs of my childhood tell me, Maybe then it'll finally be enough
