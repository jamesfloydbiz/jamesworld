Much Wisdom is  buried six feet deep
Still more is burned  with none to keep
Very little makes  it  out

If it does, its like the doves with their branch
A story and  symbols like brands on a ranch
Gold, the sun, light, darkness, hair, ashes
All means a message from past lashes

Pain encapsulated for our special brains
to capture late, You see  no longer do
the old teach the young and man has that stung
Few men, many boys  few women, many girls
So many adults not many grown ups
The cause of any quarrels and unfilled cups

It used to  be  that there comes a stage
An age where the boys go with the sage
Away from the females, where the men recount tales
The boys are injured and then go   inward
With effort, and intention there is wisdom retention
   And from this only men   return

They have  learned the lessons of life
passed down through calculated strife
      What is called a classic initiation
{  Step 1: Bonding with the Mother, then separation
-  Step 2: Get close with the Father, then cut ties
{  Step 3: Arrival of the mentor who tells no lies
—  Step 4: Apprentice to  a  hurricane
{  Step 5: Marriage  to  a queen I'll give no name

      There is    much that has been lost
         and it has come at a cost
   So if over this you fawn
go read the  story, of  Iron John
