.
   The sun has set
 The light is gone
Another day  around the sun

The world slows down
Cricket creak, birds roost
and owls go hoot hoot

Questions asked thoughts pondered
Reflection Time then  rest
Have I done  my  best?

Competed against only me?
Loved  hard, dreamed big?
Time is short, we know.

Deep breath  now rest
Let eyes   be heavy
And  body be deeply ready

Breathe slow, center self
cuddle in, rest fully
Focus on where you are

You are loved
You are yours
Now  go take
Dreamland tours
