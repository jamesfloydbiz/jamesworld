        Connection

     A spark of electricity, shocking
     Ice melting, a river flowing to the sea
     It may be silent or sing to the sky
and it has a wondrous way of making the world go round

              connection
   creates life  and prevents strife
   a sort of salve to the sorrows of now
   Many search, seeking safety  or stories
   others find it in art, nature, or self and have no worries
              connection
   is  eyes meeting and smiles shared
   it is trusting others and giving your heart
   it fills your body like sun on your back, or swimming in streams
   and necessitates  presence  and an  ability to be
              connection

Is the  medium through which all things flow
Electricity, love, temperature, joy, energy in all forms
   You see, connection  is the key
   That creates memories out of your dreams
