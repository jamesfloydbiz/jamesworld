I have what many wont
and yet I lament as I confide

You have it all they have replied
In their own ways and fonts

      Find peace, Pursue Passions
Wisdoms I know, but stray from,

Ambitions pull me out as they come
As if my body is trying to use gratitude as rations

What is it that takes this knowledge deep
and stretches the serenity I find towards a greater climb

I refuse to break, the pain is unbearable
my mind fighting amidst itself

Keep going, and get better.
The top shelf is reached through years & tears
