Tail Wags, Mouth hangs open
Dog leans forward, eyes focused

Smile stretches , as dogs ears perk
The dog is seen, so it is loved


Friends chatting , one jokes
eyes meet , big laughs

Hand grasps shoulder, joke continues
The friends laugh harder as they are seen


An older woman , arms crossed
sits on a stump , away from the group

A man stands up, and walks over to chat
she is seen and included so she is loved

Birds chirp , and flit around
Roll in dirt , and perch on branches


One finds a crumb, another sees
The crumb is shared , the friends are each seen


and they are loed
