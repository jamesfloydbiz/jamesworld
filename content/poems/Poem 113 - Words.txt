Words
Letters in groups, Thoughts that play in loops
Hoops we create for ourselves to jump through
Whoops we exclaim in moments of exhiliration

Words
spells we cast on our realities
Bonds that break or make trust
flow that can create rust in relationships and
actions that show the qualities of you

Words
Are both light and heavy
They are the canvas to the paint
but boy can they carry such weight

Words
Have tradition and history
they carry wisdom between generations in stories
rule's that create protection
rejection and socialization come from their direction
They are even still used as door keys

Words
are the leaves grown to gather energy, but also create shade
thay are the green grass that grows fast
You see they are no arbitrary part of life

Words matter

They can be a voice of hope or hopelessness
They can be curiosity or anger
They can make your reality what you want it to be.


Those lazy in their words allow laziness in life
