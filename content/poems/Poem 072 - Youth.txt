               Youth
The lightness of steps
 Simpleness of Minds not yet layered with thoughts
Not wrought with the walls we put up that make
our brains a labyrinth. Causing us pain as we
get lost.

         Youth
Is the creative momentum of blanket forts or
imaginary adventures, creation of worlds as
ideas dock in our reality like ports. Details float
in simply as if birds in an aviary. There is no
pressure to create or not simply expansion.

         Youth
is when there is not yet a normal where
problems are accepted and solved with no drama
It is asking for help and calling for mama
and it is sharing your heart with your help not just pat.

         Youth
Is purity in having boundaries and caring for
others, a rarity in the adult world which is daunting.
No games with a child, but with grown ups it can be wild

         Youth
is that freedom to believe and dream to
Stretch the seams of your reality. Growth is normal
and there is no need to be formal just move through
space and time like a wormhole

         Youth
is abundance no scarcity of time or money
we look beyond those limits and our beliefs
decide what's possible. And if so then it's plausible
we take a shot no worry about embarrassment for
our minds give us no harassment

         Youth
is health and connection to nature. No need
to wait your turn to get some sun, Nutrients
full and nervous system regulated life is always
underrated

         Youth
is wonder and curiosity a life that is
full of adventure and learning. Every day is an opportunity
to ascend, sure. New information filling our brains
as we boundlessly devour every occasion.
