Go Inwards
4/24/2026

I must remind myself to go inwards

To inhale and turn my focus towards my soul, 
to exhale as I am once again connected to all things.
For my lungs to fill full with the beauty of the present
for my breath to ring in the next moment's gifts

I must remind myself to go inwards

During the very times that pull me outwards
While the world's whirlwind tries to take my peace
For I would be a coward to not defend that sanctity
Regardless of whether or not this body is leased

I must remind myself to go inwards

Because my power comes from my insides
I have all wisdom already, it's what we call intuition
What I must do is raise the sail of faith with pride
and allow energy to fill it to fruition

I must remind myself to go inwards

to search for the darkest depths of myself
to find and hold my hearts vulnerabilities 
to know my souls wealth
and to bare each of these fully

I must remind myself that going inwards

guarantees nothing. 
No easy life, no riches, or wife.
and yet it feels right.
So further inwards I will fight.
