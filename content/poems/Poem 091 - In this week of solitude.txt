         In this week of solitude
         My eyes have raised to the horizon
I see that dark clouds and lightning are beginning to collude
As predicted today is now the sci-fi, a singularity of
known human history. There is a supersonic tsunami
headed towards us, but egos have come to play. This
hesitancy sure to make us pay. However I must stand
fast that there is a way to be okay.

   So as I look towards this speeding storm that looms
I think to my loved ones, and prepare myself for the thunder
booms. I feel in my core that there is much in store
for these next four years.
   It is in these existential moments that I know over
What I will shed tears. I learn my fears and what I hold
dear. Crazy that I have so few peers in this.
   What I know I must do is write here that
   it is time to hold love & light near. Understand
   how to pursue a view even while in the dark.
