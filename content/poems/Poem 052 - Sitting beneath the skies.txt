     Sitting beneath the skies
        A wind whistling through white pines
     Searching for stillness in self
     Is a process of meandering through your mind

     Find peace  In fierce places
     and breathe deep  as reality moves around you
     A calm so true  it calls to others hearts
     Like the fading hue of the sun against the sky blue

     Slow as the world speeds, smooth
     Slide with grace through life and avoid the rat race
           Instead of looking out, look in
     Kind of like the patience it takes to hook trout
     and the moves the greats of the past took then.

  Imagine the water lapping softly on the shore
  Sleeping babies unmoving in their slumber
     There is no rush to get anywhere
     because everything  you need is here

  Find gratitude and break the wait for more
  After all, that is what leaves people poor
  Your heart is bigger, to everything It is the door
  Stillness in Self  that is what the journey is for
