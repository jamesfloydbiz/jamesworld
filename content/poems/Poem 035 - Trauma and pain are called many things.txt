      Trauma and pain are called many things
      but in the end it is  in vain
      A dark seeping poison for which light is a bane

Without healing it stacks like a pile on top
Forcing  you further and further down into the depths
Flailing and  falling, writhing  as you drop
screaming or sobbing pleading for many deaths

   Over and over, endless it  seems
Is the light even real if this is the dark?
Still harder it will get ripping the seams
of your reality and   leaving a mark

This cycle continues  forever and always
Pain begets pain   again and again
It is an easy tap, but what if there were other ways
So if I may, a moment please, a break in the rain

what if there was no weight? Only weightlessness
A flowing, fantastic feeling of a full cup
Where love begets love
