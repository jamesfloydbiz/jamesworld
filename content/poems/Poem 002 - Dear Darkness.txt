Dear Darkness,

They ask me questions
why the music?
How did you get there?
Do you know how far you'll go?
The smart ones sometimes ask
 are you sure you're okay?
They don't understand

The darkness seeps
It seeks to sicken
to rot the foundation
The only way is through
but it seems like forever,
maybe it is. And what.
Then my bones ache,
my hairs grey
Erosion
A cycle push, tire, push, tire
I am Sisyphus

I am a lone wolf
Hunting, Killing, starving
Feeling, deeply, caring unconditionally
It is a yoke, and there is but one ox.
The whip is madness, why do I even contine?
I do not quit
I wade through pain, but why?
There is no answer and no empathy
I plod on
